Size chart

Body measurements, in inches. Our kurtas are cut with about 4 inches of ease.

How to measure

  1. Bust

    Around the fullest part of your chest, tape level under your arms, over a light top.

  2. Waist

    Around your natural waist, the narrowest part, usually just above the navel.

  3. Hip

    Around the fullest part of your hips, feet together. Between two sizes? Take the larger one.
